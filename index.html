Asked at the counter, answered the same way

The stuff everybody actually asks

Does it hurt?

Yes — but in the bearable way, or nobody would have a second one. Forearms and calves are easy days; ribs and feet will get your attention. Most first-timers say the same thing on the way out: "that was it?" Big pieces get split into sessions, and you can tap out for a break whenever you need one.

Seriously — how much is it going to cost me?

We know it's the first question. Here's why you won't get a number over the phone: the same design at 3 inches and 8 inches, on a calf versus ribs, is two different tattoos and two different prices. Anybody quoting blind is guessing with your money. Come in for a free consult — ten minutes — and you leave with a real price range and a time estimate for your exact piece. No obligation, no pressure to book on the spot.

I've never done this. How does booking even work?

Easier than you think: just walk in. Bring whatever references you've got — screenshots are fine — look around, and we do the consult right then, during that visit. You get your design plan and price range on the spot. Like it? A deposit locks your tattoo date. Still thinking? Go think. The consult cost you nothing but the drive. (Rather schedule the consult ahead? Form, call, or text — same thing, just with a time attached.)

Can I just walk in today?

Yes — that's the move, honestly. Walk in, look around, and we handle the rest right then: the consult happens during your visit, no appointment needed. Want a tattoo today too? Walk-in chairs open up most days, first come, first served. Want to know who's in before you drive over? Text us at (813) 230-3544 and we'll tell you straight.

Do I need an appointment for a consult?

Nope. Consults are walk-in — come in during shop hours, look around, and we sit down and talk your piece out right then. Free, about 15–20 minutes, and you leave with a real price range. If you'd rather have a set time, book ahead with the form or a text — but you never have to.

Can you cover up this thing I regret?

Usually, yes — but it depends on how dark and dense the old piece is, and anybody who promises sight-unseen is lying to you. Bring it in. We'll look at it in person and tell you honestly what will actually cover it, before you spend a dollar.

Why do you need a deposit? Is it a scam thing?

Not a scam thing — a calendar thing. Your deposit ($250 with T, $100 with the crew) locks your artist's whole day to you: that chair turns everyone else away, and your design gets drawn before you arrive. It's nonrefundable because of that, and it comes straight off the price of your tattoo — it's not extra. Reschedule if life happens (up to two times, no drama). Just ghost us with no word? Then we're done — that's the one rule we don't bend.

Can I bring somebody with me?

Of course — bring your person. Moral support is half the fun of a consult, and for a first tattoo it settles the nerves. Just know the chair area stays work-focused on session day, and your artist makes the final call on space.

Will I see my design before tattoo day?

Yes — your artist draws it custom for you, and you'll go over it together. It's your skin; there are no surprise reveals here.

How old do I have to be? What ID?

Bring a valid government photo ID — you'll need it. Florida rules apply, and we'll confirm exactly what's needed for your age and your piece when you book.

How do I take care of it after?

Your artist walks you through it before you leave — in person, not just a printout — and aftercare is stocked right here in the shop. The short version: keep it clean, keep it moisturized, keep it out of the sun and the pool while it heals. Something looks off? Call us. We'd rather hear from you than have you Googling it at 2 AM.
